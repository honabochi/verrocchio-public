# CONTRATTO

## Commission

Complete VERROCCHIO as the reusable execution system that carries any
hackathon from a replaceable mission profile to verified submission.

Phase 1 must be operationally complete and self-proven before Phase 2 begins.
No separate submission workpiece is selected or built inside Phase 1.

## Launch window

The current event's official deadline, timezone, and track are mission inputs.
No event-specific launch window is compiled into the engine.

## Initial submission evidence

The neutral seed ledger is only a readiness scaffold. MISSION INTAKE must
dynamically compile the current official rules into four to ten proof gates,
and a replan must preserve any completed proof.

## DI SUA MANO

The human owns:

- WHY: purpose, taste, and the final product promise.
- NO: scope rejection and the veto on irreversible operations.
- FIRMA: payment, publishing, personal data, contract changes, and final submission.

The workshop owns HOW.

## Decision materials

- `AFFRESCO`: irreversible; synchronous human FIRMA required.
- `SECCO`: reversible; proceed now and collect for later review.
- `GESSO`: low-risk groundwork; proceed autonomously and log it.
- `FERMO`: any participant may hold the work.
- `PENTIMENTO`: a visible change to the contract or purpose.

## Scope ratchet

After this contract, scope may shrink without a PENTIMENTO. It may not expand without one.
