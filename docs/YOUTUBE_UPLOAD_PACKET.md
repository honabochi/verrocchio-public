# VERROCCHIO YouTube Upload Packet

This packet prepares the public demo upload without creating an account,
signing in, uploading, or publishing. The Owner performs every account-facing
and publication step.

## Recommended public identity

- Reuse the Owner's existing Google Account; a second Google Account is not
  required.
- Create or select a separate YouTube channel named **NoBu Builds** so
  the public channel identity does not need to match the Google Account name.
- Suggested handles, subject to live availability:
  - `@nobubuilds`
  - `@nobu-builds`
  - `@nobuildslab`
- Before upload, inspect the channel name, handle, profile picture, About page,
  and visible links. Remove personal names, photos, email addresses, and
  unrelated links.

The Owner reports that the channel has been created. The selected handle and
public profile still require an Owner readback before publication; do not store
the account email or private Studio identity in this repository.

## Prepared video package

- Storyboard and final narration: `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT_EN.md`
- Timed English captions: `docs/DEMO_CAPTIONS_EN.srt`
- Target runtime: **2:35–2:45**
- Capture: **2560×1440** recommended
- Export: **1920×1080 MP4**
- Visuals: product and host screen only; no face is required
- Audio: clear English narration; no background music
- Narration may be the Owner's voice or a synthetic voice whose license permits
  this public use. Confirm the selected voice service's terms before export.

Do not record until the synchronized frozen revision is anonymously reachable
and its hosted WebMCP path has been verified. The current Site is public, but
Version 25 precedes the latest local release packet and is not yet the final
recording target.

## Owner upload sequence

1. Sign in to YouTube with the intended existing Google Account.
2. Select the **NoBu Builds** channel.
3. Confirm its public identity exposes no personal name, photo, email, or
   unrelated links.
4. Upload the final MP4 as **Private**.
5. Paste the title and description, then replace the verified live-demo URL.
6. Select the audience and other settings above.
7. Upload the timed English SRT file.
8. Wait for HD processing and YouTube's checks to finish.
9. Watch the complete private video at normal speed with captions on.
10. Check every frame for notifications, profile images, account identifiers,
    browser history, private task names, secrets, and unrelated tabs.
11. Confirm audio, captions, runtime, source link, live URL, and chapter times.
12. Change visibility to **Public** only after the Owner's final publication
    confirmation.
13. Open the public URL in a signed-out/private browser window and verify video,
    audio, captions, description links, and 1080p playback.
14. Copy the verified public YouTube URL into the Devpost submission draft.

## Stop conditions

Stop before publication if any of the following is true:

- the live demo still requires authentication;
- the video shows personal or secret-bearing information;
- a displayed WebMCP result cannot be reproduced from the hosted build;
- captions no longer match the final edit;
- the runtime reaches three minutes;
- YouTube reports a copyright or processing problem;
- the public channel identity is not the identity the Owner intended;
- a numeric improvement claim lacks a passing comparison receipt.

## Final receipt to preserve

After publication, record only these non-secret facts in the submission packet:

- final public video URL;
- publication visibility confirmed as Public;
- runtime and 1080p availability;
- captions present and readable;
- final public demo URL tested signed out;
- source link opens without authentication;
- date and time of the final readback;
- any known limitation shown honestly in the demo or description.

Do not record Google Account identifiers, channel-owner email, cookies, OAuth
data, recovery information, or private Studio screenshots.
